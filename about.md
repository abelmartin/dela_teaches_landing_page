    <h2>Hello, I'm Dela</h2>
    <p>I’m an English teacher with over 10 years of experience teaching adults and kids. I hold a Master’s in TESOL (Teaching English to Speakers of Other Languages) and have taught English in schools, community centers, and online.</p>
    <p>After taking time off to raise my two boys, I returned to teaching English in 2019. I helped transition my church’s English program online during the pandemic and began working with students on reading and pronunciation.</p>
    <p>I love teaching and seeing my students read, speak, and grow in English. Outside of class, I love reading books for book club, going for runs with my local running club, and spending time in the garden.</p>

      <h4 style="color: var(--primary-color); margin-bottom: 0.5rem;">My Teaching Philosophy</h4>
      <p style="margin-bottom: 0;">Language learning happens best when you feel supported, encouraged, and engaged with meaningful topics. We focus on real spoken clarity, active reading comprehension, and natural conversation flow.</p>
